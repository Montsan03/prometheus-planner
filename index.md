# Prometheus Planner — Support

**Prometheus Planner** is a classic paper-style digital planner for iPhone, iPad and Mac: daily spread with ABC-123 priorities, weekly compass, monthly master list, mission and roles, with optional sync to Apple Calendar and Reminders.

## FAQ
**How do I enable reminders for appointments and tasks?**
Settings → Recordatorios (Reminders). Choose lead time for appointments and the daily digest hour for tasks.

**How does sync with Apple Reminders work?**
Enable it in Settings → Sincronización (Sync). Tasks created in Prometheus appear in the "Prometheus" list in Reminders and vice versa.

**Where are my backups?**
Settings → Respaldo (Backup). Export a `.goblin` backup file or restore from a previous one. Note: backup files are not encrypted — store them somewhere safe.
